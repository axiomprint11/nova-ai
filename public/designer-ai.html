Match Logic

Write how you want jobs matched to designers, in your own words — who takes what, who to protect, who to grow, what to avoid. Nova scores every designer with its built-in rules first (history with the product, strong sides, hardness and creativity against their levels, newer designers on common easy work, hours, load), then reads what you wrote here and moves the scores where your rules apply. Each change shows on the pick as "your rule: …".

Overload Logic

When nobody can take a job. Nova's own test: every designer who takes jobs is at their Max active, or the only ones with room are off shift. Then the job stays in the queue as it is, the row is marked Everyone is busy, and the people below get one email per job per day — the designers pick it up themselves. Write here when you want that to happen (or not), in your own words; the model reads it on every Match.

When everyone is busy the job is left in the queue, tagged on the Test Data row and logged in History — no email is sent.

What Nova scores on its own

  • History — did this product in 180 days (+5 a job, up to +30), else the same category (+2 a job, up to +15).
  • Strong sides — a word from the designer's list found in the product, category, proof type or job name (+12 each, up to +36).
  • Tier 2 — a Tier 2 job: +15 for a Tier 2 designer, −20 otherwise.
  • Hardness — the job's 0–10 (category, die cut, wrap, variable data, Tier 2, expedite) against the designer's Hard jobs level: −6 per point over; +6 for a hard job within their level.
  • Creativity — the job's 0–10 (design fee, "Work with Our Designers", design notes) rewards a high Creative level and costs a low one (−15 when 2+ below).
  • Newer designers (under 60 jobs in 180 days) — +15 on a common, easy, non-creative product; −10 on a hard or creative one.
  • Hours — off shift or day off −45; leaving within the hour −25; within 90 minutes on a hard job −15.
  • Load — −3 per active job above the lightest designer (up to −30); −40 at their max. Fairness — −4 per pick made here in the last 2 hours.

DesignersFaces, titles and active jobs come from the CRM. Strong sides, Tier 2 and limits are kept in Nova — the matcher reads them.

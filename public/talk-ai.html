Built-in phone rules

Always on, in the code:

  • Short spoken sentences; no lists or links read out
  • Answers in the caller's language
  • Order details only for a recognised number (see Callers we know), or after the caller confirms the email or ZIP on the account (with the order number if the number is unknown) — three tries
  • Prices only from the website calculator
  • Never takes card numbers or payments
  • Past-due orders escalated by email, as in the chat

Each account manager's own NovaAI

One NovaAI number per account manager, doing two jobs:

  • Their missed calls — clients who call it (or whose calls they don't take) get NovaAI instead of voicemail: it greets them, tries to help, and takes a message for them.
  • Their assistant — when they call it from their own phone: “Hi Armine! You have 2 new calls. How can I help today?” It reads out their calls and messages, looks up clients, projects and jobs, prices work and emails them notes.

Their clients on the main line can get their NovaAI too, with no extra number.

Good to know

  • Hours, the shared rules and the caller check apply to every line.
  • Ring first works during regular hours. With “press 1 to take it” on, their voicemail can’t take the call: if they don’t press 1, NovaAI answers.
  • Dialpad: to send their unanswered Dialpad calls to NovaAI, set their Dialpad “unanswered calls” forwarding to their NovaAI number, and leave Ring first off.
  • Their assistant trusts their phone when the carrier confirms the number (most cell phones). Set a PIN for the other times, or to always ask.
  • A voice of their own needs Voice allowed under the ElevenLabs agent's Security → Overrides.
  • Try it lets you test any line as any caller.
